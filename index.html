Controls

Tap a key, then press the new key you want. Esc always pauses.

Keyboard
Touch
  • Left side: drag anywhere for a floating stick.
  • Shoot: hold the big button, release in the green.
  • Pass / Steal: second button. On offense, hold a direction to pick the target.
  • Dribble moves: flick on the right side of the screen. Left/right flick is a crossover, up is a hesitation, down is a step-back. Flick twice fast for a behind-the-back or a spin.
  • Layup / Dunk / Alley: small buttons by the thumb. Tap Dunk twice fast for a cool dunk. Alley throws a lob to a teammate near the rim; tap it twice fast to throw it off the glass to yourself.
  • Turbo, Switch, T/O: small buttons by your thumb. Switch changes your defender.
